How a chemical supply starts

Dosing is decided by the water, not the price list

The same antiscalant that protects one membrane will foul another. Four steps before anything is quoted.

01

Water analysis

Send your feedwater report — or we arrange sampling. Hardness, silica, TDS and microbial load decide the chemistry.

02

Dosage calculation

We work out the dose rate against your flow and recovery, so you buy the quantity the plant actually consumes.

03

Supply & dosing setup

Chemicals delivered with the dosing pumps and accessories matched to them, not sourced separately.

04

Monitoring

Test kits and instruments so your own team can verify performance between service visits.
