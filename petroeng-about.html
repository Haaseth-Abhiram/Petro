Who we are

A specialised sourcing and supply partner

Petro Engineering Private Limited is a specialised sourcing and supply partner for oil & gas, petrochemical and industrial analyser systems.

We support EPC contractors, system integrators and end users by supplying certified equipment, precision fittings, analyser consumables and shelter components from globally recognised OEMs.

Our work focuses on understanding client requirements and providing suitable solutions through careful planning, technical knowledge and industry experience. We connect approved manufacturers with project requirements while ensuring quality and cost efficiency.
