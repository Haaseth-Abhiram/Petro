FAQs

Common questions

Which international standards do you supply against?

We source from approved and reputable manufacturers, ensuring compliance with international standards including API, ASTM, ASME, ISO and IEC. Tubing is supplied to ASTM A269 and A213. The applicable standard and certification scope is confirmed in writing at order stage.

Do you supply documentation and material traceability?

Yes. Material Test Certificates are verified against the supplied goods, and we coordinate third-party inspection, FAT and SAT where the project requires it. Documentation is assembled and checked before dispatch rather than collected afterwards.

Can you handle a multi-vendor package as a single order?

That is the core of our procurement support. We manage RFQs across multiple vendors, align them technically and commercially, expedite against your schedule, and consolidate shipments — so you deal with one point of contact instead of several suppliers.

What materials and pressure ratings are available?

Fittings and valves are available in SS316, SS316L, carbon steel, brass, duplex and specialist alloys, sized 1/8" to 1" (6–25 mm) and rated up to 6000 psi, with needle valves available to 10,000 psi. Full details are on the products page.

Do you export internationally?

Yes. We provide export-grade packing and preservation, complete export documentation, and freight coordination with global delivery support. Tubing is supplied in 10–200 m coils, spooled or wrapped for export.

How do I request a quotation?

Send your line list, datasheet or RFQ through the enquiry form, email sales@petroeng.in, or call +91 96521 11021. We review the specification and come back with a compliant, costed proposal including lead times.
